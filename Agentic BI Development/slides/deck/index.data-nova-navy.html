02 · The hinge
Advisory or blocking?
Advisory
Instructions and skills.
The agent decides whether to read them. Nothing fails if it does not.
Blocking
Hooks and gates.
The agent cannot walk past them. A failing check stops the build.
A knowledge file is advice. Any tool can skip it, on any run. A check cannot be skipped.

The brief

A headline balance card that flips colour from surplus to deficit
A trend by month
A breakdown by partner country or commodity
A slicer to switch the measure
