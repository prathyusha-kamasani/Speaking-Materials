Overview

What this report tells you

Is Britain selling more than it buys, by how much, and how has it moved?

The Balance answers that in one glance. It opens with a plain-English verdict and a one-line summary, shows the four headline numbers, and then lets you see the trade gap and how it has behaved year on year.

The thinking

The idea behind the design

One sentence, five seconds

Britain buys more than it sells. An executive reads the verdict and the four numbers and knows the position without touching anything. thirty seconds more and the trend confirms it.

The gap is the story

A deficit is naturally a gap, so the trend chart draws it as the shaded space between exports and imports. drawn, not stated. and the year-by-year bars show it has persisted.

Colour carries meaning

Exports and surplus are green, imports and deficit are clay. Colour appears only where a number is signed, so the story is legible at a glance without a legend.

Honest by design

One consistent trade basis, the source named on the page. A goods-vs-services split was left out because it isn't reliable at this level and would imply precision the data can't support.

The report

What's on each page

The Balance (main page)

  • Verdict + summary line: a plain-English statement of the position, with the deficit figure picked out in clay.
  • Period caption: the exact 12-month window shown.
  • Four headline cards: Total Trade (the hero number), Exports (green), Imports (clay) and Trade Balance. the balance card's colour flips with the sign, and each card carries a small 12-month trend.
  • Deficit / surplus chip: a one-word status.
  • Exports vs imports: the two series over recent years, with the deficit shown as the shaded gap between them.
  • The deficit, year by year: the balance for each recent year, latest year emphasised.
  • Source line: the report's own provenance footer.

Year detail (drill into a year)

Right-click a year and choose drill-through to open a focused view of that year: the same three headline figures re-scoped to the year, a month-by-month exports-vs-imports trend in calendar order, and a table of the monthly numbers behind it. A back arrow returns you to the main page.

Hover tooltip

Hovering a point on the charts shows a compact card with that year's exports, imports and balance.

Data

Data & sources

  • Where the numbers come from: ONS Monthly Trade (MRET, seasonally adjusted) for the headline totals, and HMRC Overseas Trade for goods detail.
  • Consistency: a single, consistent trade basis throughout, so exports, imports and the balance always reconcile.
  • Period: always the rolling latest 12 months, anchored on the most recent month with data. never a stale or future month.
  • Refresh: every number, and the verdict, colour and wording that depend on it, updates automatically when the data refreshes.
Assurance

How we know it is right

  • The four headline figures reproduce the published gov.uk UK Trade in Numbers headline to within 0.005%, checked against the live report.
  • Every visual on every page was confirmed to return data on the live report.
  • The report was reviewed for accessibility (colour contrast and colour-blind safety) and for a clean, consistent layout.
Transparency

Known limitations

None outstanding. The report's figures, colours (including the Year-detail table's export-green / import-clay value columns) and layout all render as designed on the live report.
