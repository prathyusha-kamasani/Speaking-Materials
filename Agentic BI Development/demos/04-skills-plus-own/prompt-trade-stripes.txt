I need a one-page Power BI report that shows the whole history of the UK trade balance at a glance.

Who it is for
Anyone who sees it for five seconds, on a screen or printed: they should come away knowing whether the UK has mostly bought more than it sold, and when that changed. Analysts should be able to check the numbers behind it.

The data
Use the semantic model SM_UKT_Import in the Fabric workspace "UK Trade Data Reports".
- The balance: [Trade Balance (GBP bn)] by 'Date'[Year Month]. 'Trade Total Monthly' holds one basis (balance of payments, seasonally adjusted) and one period type (monthly), so the measure needs no basis or period filter. Never sum across Basis or Period Type if more are ever added.
- 'Date' is a reserved word in DAX, so always quote it.
- Use every month that has a value. Leave out any row with a blank Year Month, so no "(Blank)" column appears.
- Never change or delete anything already in the model. New measures this page needs (the headline sentence, the annotation labels and values, the stripe colour as a hex, the period label) are added as new objects through model_requests.yaml, each verified against the live model.
- Text boxes take no visual filters (G-19), so each text measure applies its own window in DAX.

What to build
1. Trade stripes: one column per month for the whole history, columns with no gap, no axes and no gridlines, each column coloured by the sign of the balance from a model measure returning a hex. The shape of the history is the message. Declared deviation G-36: the no-gap setting and the per-month column width are emitted but unverified until the first canary render; check them there.
2. A one-sentence headline that states what the stripes show, computed by a model measure, not typed.
3. Three or four short annotations at turning points the data itself shows (for example the first month of a long deficit run, or the largest deficit), placed at fixed positions. Each names the month and the value, both computed by model measures.
4. A small key: what each colour means, and the period covered, from model measures.

Done means
- The number of columns equals the number of months with a value.
- Every column's colour comes from a measure, so it still holds after a refresh.
- Each annotation's month and value match a DAX query against the model.
- The live-render check and the render review pass on the final canary: all text fully visible, readable at a glance, no scrollbar on the stripes.

Constraints
- Portrait page, 1080 by 1920, shown fit to width.
- Colours from the report theme, not typed into visuals (the stripe colour measure returns theme hex values).
- Native Power BI visuals only.

Out of scope
Drill-through, other pages, partner or commodity breakdowns, services and FDI data, and any change to existing model objects.
