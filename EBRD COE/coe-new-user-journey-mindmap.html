A new user's journey in a CoE-enabled organisation

A new user joins and needs to create a report. At each step the CoE has already set the rules, so they always know what to do next.

Drag to move around, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a stage to expand or collapse it. Use the buttons to expand all, collapse all, or fit the whole map on screen.
At every step there is a rule, and the rule tells you what to do next. That is why a new user can get from joining to a published report without asking around. Discipline at the core, flexibility at the edge.
